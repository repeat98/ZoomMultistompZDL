Effects on the pedal

Install, update and remove this project's effects over USB, without Zoom Effect Manager. Tested on the MS-70CDR with firmware 2.10. Zoom's own effects are never removed here; missing ones can be put back from Zoom's updater, below. After a change, power-cycle the pedal to load it. Don't touch the pedal or the cable while a write runs.

Connect, then read the pedal.

Zoom's effects, from Zoom's updater

Put back Zoom's own effects without Effect Manager. Choose the firmware updater from Zoom: the MS-70CDR 2.10 Mac updater (.zip) (it works for this on any computer; don't unzip it), or FS.bin from it. Other versions are on Zoom's MS-70CDR support page ↗. The effects are unpacked and prepared in this browser, the way Effect Manager installs them; nothing is uploaded. Only effects missing from the pedal, or newer than the pedal's copy, can be installed. Effect Manager's library can have newer versions than the updater.

For files made by the NAM and cab loaders (NAM… and C… files). Other files are refused.

NAM / Cab

Amp captures
ⓘ

A .nam capture becomes its own effect, converted in this browser and installed over USB (MS-70CDR). A2 Lite captures only. The pedal shows it as NAM- plus the first 5 letters of the short name. Replacing a capture keeps its slot, so patches that used it play the new one.

or

Cabs
ⓘ

A cab IR (.wav, 44.1 or 48 kHz, up to 2 s) is fitted into its own CabIR effect, using 10 of ~228 DSP. The pedal shows it as CAB- plus the first 5 letters of the name.
